# Smart Map Vision

Transform mapping for Africa into a trusted public-safety and navigation platform.

## Mission

Connecting Africa through intelligent navigation, trusted public services, and AI-powered safety.

## Principles

1. **Safety first** — SOS, emergency numbers, family protection, safer routes
2. **Trusted places** — verified government, hospital, school, and business badges
3. **Community truth** — report hazards with GPS, media, and AI summaries
4. **Offline capable** — PWA + background sync for unreliable connectivity
5. **Pan-African scale** — Ghana launch, then all 54 countries

## Brand

Smart Map — Explore • Connect • Stay Safe
